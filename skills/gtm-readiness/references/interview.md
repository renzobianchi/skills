# Interview question bank

Two or three per call. Every pillar question has a number behind it; "consistently" without a number is a no.

## Round 1, the four pillars

- "Can you consistently get in front of your ideal customers?" → "Of your last ten first meetings or signups, how many came from the segment you want?"
- "Can you consistently close deals with your ICP?" → "Of the last ten qualified deals, how many closed, and were they the same kind of customer?"
- "Can you consistently get customers to value?" → "How many of the last ten reached the first real outcome, and how long did it take?"
- "Do they stick around?" → "Of customers from a year ago, how many are still paying? Who left, and what were they using before?"
- "Are you trying to build a big platform in year one, or sequencing your way to one?"
- "Could you explain your product in one sentence?" (record it verbatim; it is the positioning hypothesis)

## Round 2, the segment

- "What kind of company, and who inside it champions the purchase?"
- "What workflow do they buy you for?" → "What were they doing for that workflow before you?"
- "What is the problem, in the words the champion uses on the first call?"
- "Who was easiest to close? Who gets the most joy from the product? Who shares it with peers?"
- "Who churned, and what did they use before you?" → a churned customer who was happy with their alternative was never in the ICP.
- "Does anyone actually own solving this? Is it anyone's job? Does anyone have budget?"
- "Do they already have the use case, or do you have to convince them to start doing the activity?"

## Round 3, the plan

- "How many segments are you selling to right now, and with how many people on GTM?"
- "Do you have a distribution advantage: a community, an audience, virality, a large raise?"
- "What is time to value: minutes, weeks, or quarters? Is there an implementation?"
- "What is the ACV range, and who signs?"
- "What do you think your biggest point of differentiation is?" → "Would you guarantee that outcome?"
- Customer interview trio to run after the audit: "How did you hear about us in the first place?" "Why did you book a meeting with us?" "Why did you buy our product?"
