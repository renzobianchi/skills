# startup-positioning

A guided positioning interview for a B2B product you do not own. Run it with a founder on a call or over chat: it gates on whether they can commit to one segment, reads their website first, interviews in four rounds, builds three to five candidate strategies with a thesis and risks each, and delivers one positioning doc at three lengths that a homepage can be written from.

```
/startup-positioning https://example.com
/startup-positioning "Cravel" quick
```

Pairs with `homepage-messaging` (structure and hero from the doc) and `language-market-fit` (copy in the prospect's words).
